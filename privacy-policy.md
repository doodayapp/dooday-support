# DooDay Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 20, 2026**

DooDay respects your privacy. The app is designed to provide a simple and private way to track bowel movements and set reminders.

## Information We Collect

DooDay does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

Information you enter into DooDay, including your bowel movement history and reminder settings, is used only to provide the app's tracking and reminder features.

DooDay does not require you to create an account.

## Data Storage

Your DooDay tracking information is stored locally on your device.

DooDay does not operate a server that receives or stores your bowel movement history.

If you delete the app, locally stored DooDay data may also be deleted from your device.

## Notifications

DooDay may ask for permission to send notifications so that it can provide the reminders you choose to set.

You can change or disable notification permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

## Third-Party Sharing

DooDay does not sell or share your bowel movement tracking information with advertisers or other third parties.

DooDay does not use your tracking information for advertising.

## Children's Privacy

DooDay does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Medical Disclaimer

DooDay is intended for personal tracking and general wellness purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you have concerns about constipation or your health, please contact a qualified healthcare professional.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if DooDay's features or privacy practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
