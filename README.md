Thanks for using DooDay! DooDay is a cute, simple way to track bowel movements, view your history, and set reminders.

## Using DooDay

- Tap “I Pooped!” to save a bowel movement.
- Open Poop History to view your calendar and add or remove entries.
- In Reminder Settings, choose when you’d like a reminder and allow notifications. You can change notification permission in your device’s Settings.

## In the next update

- Tap “Things I Took” to log an entry immediately. A name, amount, and notes are optional.
- Tap a calendar date to see, add, edit, or remove that day’s entries, including optional notes and times.
- Choose a reminder interval from 1–10 days.
- Optional tips are available under Reminder Settings → Support DooDay. Tracking features do not require a tip. Apple handles purchases.

Your diary is saved on your device and no account is required. Deleting the app may delete its locally stored diary.
